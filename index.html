The stride

Half of every turn, flat on the ground.

Each foot traces a closed loop as the crank turns. The bottom of that loop is nearly a straight line, so for about half of each revolution the foot pushes along the ground at a constant height and the body rides level. The bronze line is the real path, solved live from the linkage.

The geometry

Eleven lengths. One gait.

Theo Jansen found these proportions by evolving thousands of candidate linkages in simulation. Change any one of them and the flat part of the stride bends. The tags on the model show where each length lives.

Jansen units. This build prints at 2 mm per unit. a, l: frame offsets · m: crank radius.
